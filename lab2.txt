Answer written questions 1 and 2 here.

1. SFML's coordinate system works with (0,0) is at the top-left and y increases as it goes downwards, instead of y is upwards in normal math coordinates.
The calculation of the angle is in the opposite direction of each other for normal coordinates and SFML coordinates.
This is surprising because of the inverted y-axis for adding the velocity towards them which requires a negative value if to convert to normal math coordinates. 

2. This design makes the codebase more readable and separates responsibilities.
This follows the MVC pattern (Model, View, Controller) where the InputSummary is a controller and GameState stores the models and controls the view with draw.
